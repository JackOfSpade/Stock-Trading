## Data-provenance note

**Correlations, volatilities and hedge ratios:** IBKR `get_price_history`, one-year daily closes (`period=ONE_YEAR`, `step=ONE_DAY`, RTH only), window 2025-08-01 → 2026-07-31, ~250–251 aligned daily log-return observations per pair; Pearson correlation and OLS hedge ratio computed in-session. FMP's `chart` and `quote` endpoints returned `ACCESS DENIED` (plan-gated) for every parameterization tried, so FMP contributed no price data this cycle.

**LIVE MCP DEFECT FOUND — `get_price_history` returns SHUFFLED series under parallel batching.** When multiple `get_price_history` calls were issued in a single batched message, responses were silently **transposed between contract_ids** — the call made with GM's contract_id returned Tesla's series, and in a separate batch MCK↔REGN, GS↔MS and COF↔AXP were each pairwise swapped (4 of 10 tickers in that batch). Every response was well-formed and nothing errored. Detected by range-checking each returned series against the ticker's known price level. **Mitigation applied across all 51 tickers used in this screen:** every series was either re-fetched **sequentially** (one call per message) and compared against the batched result, or independently corroborated — the carry-forward batch's last closes were cross-checked against separately web-sourced prices for all ten names, and PANW was verified against a direct single-call fetch. No correlation reported above rests on an unverified batched fetch. **This should be reported to W5/D3 as an infrastructure finding: any routine that batches `get_price_history` calls is at risk of silently analyzing the wrong instrument.**

**Other data notes.** CRWD, SCCO and NFLX returned 250 bars beginning 2025-08-04 rather than 251 from 2025-08-01 (IBKR window quirk, not an error); IGV returned two years despite `period=ONE_YEAR` and was truncated to the common window. NFLX was checked for split artifacts (a 2026 split is reported) — no single-day move above 40% exists in the saved series, the largest being 13.8% on 2026-02-27, so IBKR's history is split-adjusted. July percentage changes throughout use **first-July-trading-day close → 2026-07-31 close**; where a source quotes a 6/30 baseline the figure differs (notably AMD: −12.0% on the July-1 basis, −18.0% off its 6/30 close of $580.91) and both are given where material.
