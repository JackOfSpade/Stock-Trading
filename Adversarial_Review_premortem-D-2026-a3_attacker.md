## Weaknesses identified

1. **[Tier 1] Claimed calibration-detection mechanism does not exist in the artifact's own indicator set** — The two-layer control's stated partial offset for the 5x per-thesis blast-radius increase is: "With ordinal conviction now recorded per entry, size-vs-outcome becomes a directly measurable calibration series, consumed by the Section 4 indicator portfolio." None of these measures, aggregates, or otherwise "consumes" a conviction-tier-vs-outcome calibration series — no such indicator is defined anywhere in Section 4 or Section 6. This is the artifact's single named justification for why the new regime is not strictly worse than the old one on the 2.13/2.26 overconfidence dimension, and it points at a mechanism the document itself never builds. This is the canonical Tier 1 pattern: mitigation language that does not actually bind anything.
   ANCHOR: "size-vs-outcome becomes a directly measurable calibration series, consumed by the Section 4 indicator portfolio. This is a detection improvement, not a magnitude mitigation."

2. **[Tier 1] "Still mechanical, still bias-independent" is asserted for the CaR envelopes without any stated enforcement mechanism** — Every other numerically-enforced rule in this document is explicit about its enforcement method: the dual-signal typing rule is "mechanism-enforced via date comparison," the correlation-bucket assignment is "computed by classical-method delegation," and the metric-immutability auto-invalidation is "mechanism-enforced via classical-method delegation." The per-name (≤10%) and per-strategy (≤75%) Capital-at-Risk envelopes — which the document explicitly leans on as the surviving "mechanical, bias-independent" half of the two-layer control, replacing the flat 2% cap's undisputed mechanical property — are never given any such computation or enforcement description. Given that the per-name cap must sum across tranches accumulated over years (exactly the composition scrutiny this cycle calls for), and the whole "outer layer" framing depends on this being a hard, judgment-proof check rather than a self-reported number, the absence of a stated enforcement mechanism is a load-bearing gap, not a cosmetic omission.
   ANCHOR: "OUTER layer — still mechanical, still bias-independent: the hard envelopes — per-name aggregate CaR ≤ 10% of strategy portfolio value, summed across all of a name's tranches... These are versioned policy, not judgment"

3. **[Tier 1] Self-containment break — unreproduced external empirical claim used as load-bearing evidence for the residual's trend** — The RESIDUAL paragraph closes with an appeal to an outside document's findings to argue the 5x blast-radius increase "is not shrinking": "Per `AI_Trading_Foundation.md`'s A2 2026 findings, 2.13 (miscalibration) is flat and 2.18 (instruction adherence over capital preservation) is STRENGTHENED." ## Reasoning

The cycle's central question is whether the two-layer control (mechanical outer envelope + judgment-dependent inner budget) genuinely re-mitigates what the flat 2% cap covered, or merely re-labels the same exposure at a looser bound while claiming equivalence it can't support. The artifact is candid about the headline number (5x blast-radius increase, honestly computed and consistently applied) and candid about the inner layer's self-attack limitation (it explicitly concedes the mandatory adversarial-attack-on-size is a same-model check subject to the same ~70% structural residual as its own counter-argument entry criterion — I did not count that as a defect, because disclosing a limitation honestly is not itself a limitation). Where it fails is in the two places it reaches for something to *offset* that candidly-disclosed residual: (a) a "detection improvement" it says is "consumed by the Section 4 indicator portfolio," when Section 4's own five-item definition, given in full in this same document, contains no such consumer; and (b) an "OUTER layer — still mechanical, still bias-independent" characterization that every comparable rule elsewhere in the document backs up with a stated enforcement method, but this one does not. Both are load-bearing for the reader's ability to trust the residual accounting, and both fail on the artifact's own internal evidence — not on anything I had to go outside the file to check. The A2-2026-findings citation (finding 3) is a slightly different failure mode: it is not internally contradicted, it is simply unverifiable from what I was permitted to read, which the task instructions treat as a Tier 1 self-containment failure in its own right when the missing information is load-bearing (here, for "this residual is not shrinking").

I did not treat the following as defects: the 5x/~4x multiple framing itself (arithmetically correct and consistently applied); the fact that a single first tranche could be sized up to the full 10% envelope (the artifact discloses this plainly as the mechanism behind the 5x figure, not hidden); the streak-math / Rev-40-unbounded-tranching history (already reconciled in-text, with the 10% envelope correctly framed as the first re-imposition of an aggregate bound since Rev 40, not a regression); and the still-open cycle-5 residuals (W5, W7, alpha-test calibration to the modal null-alpha case) that the revision header explicitly scopes out and that Known Limitation 16's new paragraph explicitly declines to resolve rather than silently ignores. Manufacturing findings against material the artifact itself flags as out-of-scope-this-cycle would not be a genuine defect in this revision.
