2. **[Tier 1] The declared "5× looser" / "10%-per-name" residual is not the quantity the artifact says it is** — The Cross-constraint flowing limitation states the outer envelope is "a 5× looser bound than the retired 2%/4% figures (2% → 10% per name)" and that "worst-case per-thesis loss ... rose from the retired 2%-per-leg (4%-per-pair) figure to the 10%-per-name envelope." Both figures are wrong on the artifact's own definitions. First, a pair touches two names, each individually capped at 10% CaR (per the eligibility rule's own text: "applied per NAME... since a pair touches two names and each is individually subject to the 10% cap"), so worst-case per-thesis loss under the new regime is bounded near 10%+10% = ~20% of strategy portfolio value, not the 10% figure the artifact reports as "the" per-thesis bound — understating the residual it is trying to honestly quantify by roughly half. Second, the "5×" ratio (2%→10%) is computed as if CaR equals notional for both legs, but the same eligibility rule defines CaR asymmetrically: "the long leg's CaR is the full position notional (no stop-loss, so no discount); the short leg's CaR is notional × the strategy's mandatory short stop distance" (25%). For a name held as the short leg, a 10% CaR ceiling permits up to 10%/0.25 = 40% of portfolio in actual notional exposure — an ~20× loosening in real position-size terms versus the retired 2%-per-leg cap, not 5×. The artifact's central quantified-residual claim — the one this cycle exists to test for honesty — is internally inconsistent with its own CaR definition two paragraphs earlier in the same section. ANCHOR: "a 5× looser bound than the retired 2%/4% figures (2% → 10% per name)" / "the short leg's CaR is notional × the strategy's mandatory short stop distance"

ANCHOR: "the same single Claude session producing the thesis runs the counter-argument before commitment" / "Self-reference closure: the only at-entry discriminator on whether the pair narrative is over-fit is Claude's own assessment, which is biased by 2.4"

## Reasoning

The cycle's central question is whether the rev 6 redraft's replacement of the retired flat 2% cap — a two-layer control of a mechanical outer envelope plus a judgment-dependent inner risk budget — is honestly characterized, and whether the inner layer is attackable by anything but the same judgment that set it. Three findings go directly to that question and are Tier 1 because each one voids the redraft's central claim rather than merely weakening it:

- Finding 1 is the most serious: the artifact names a specific control (the size-attack extension of entry criterion 2) as compensating for three specific biases, in the same breath citing evidence that the control does not do this — and actively worsens one of the three. This is not a residual honestly disclosed; it is a mitigation claim contradicted by the artifact's own supporting citation, sitting in the load-bearing eligibility-rule text that every downstream section leans on.
- Finding 2 is the redraft's headline quantified claim (the "5×"/"10%-per-name" figures) checked against the redraft's own CaR formula two paragraphs prior, and found inconsistent by roughly 2× (per-thesis) to 4× (per-name notional on the short leg). Since honest quantification of the residual is the explicit reason this cycle exists, a wrong headline number is not a stylistic nitpick — it is the artifact failing the one thing it was redrafted to do.
- Finding 3 answers the trigger's explicit question about attackability of the inner layer directly from the artifact's own words: the size-attack is run by the same session, and the artifact elsewhere (2.4 discussion) already concedes same-session self-review is weight-level-bias-exposed and only ~30% effective. Nothing else touches the number before an order is placed.

I did not count the following as defects, deliberately: (a) the residual cycle-5 items (W3, W5, W6, W7) that the revision header explicitly marks out of scope for this redraft — re-flagging them here would not be reviewing rev 6 on its own merits; (b) the per-strategy 75% envelope being large in absolute terms — a large but honestly-labeled envelope is not itself theater, only the failure to quantify its multiple (finding 5) is a defect; (c) the general residual-probability-not-reduced framing in the Cross-constraint flowing limitation ("the magnitude bound is looser and less independent... Probability-of-correctness is unaddressed") — this is exactly the kind of honest residual disclosure the artifact should be making, and I did not fault it for conceding a limitation it explicitly owns; only the specific numeric and self-citation defects above, where the disclosure itself is wrong or the mitigation claim is self-contradicted, rise to Tier 1. Finding 6 was kept at Tier 3 specifically because, despite the letter of the self-containment instruction, I never actually hit a wall that required outside information — it is a documentation mismatch, not a functional block, and I did not want to inflate it to Tier 1 to appear rigorous.
