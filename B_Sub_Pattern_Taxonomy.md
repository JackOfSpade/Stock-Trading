## Sub_pattern canonical vocabulary (controlled set — STANDARD going forward)

**Established 2026-06-21** by the one-time `events.decision_log.sub_pattern` normalization (W5, note "sub_pattern normalization"; full old→new mapping in the W5 run log below). This block is the **authoritative controlled vocabulary** for the `events.decision_log.sub_pattern` field. **The routine that logs Strategy-B NO-GOs (D2) MUST emit these tokens** — no free-text, no casing variants (`sub-pattern 1` / `Sub-Pattern 1` → `SP1`), no composite blobs (`SP6-negative+SP4c+PatternN` → `SP6 [overlay: SP4c+PatternN]`). W5 conforms any drift.

**Token grammar**: `<PRIMARY>[<variant-letter>][ (candidate)][ [overlay: <P2>+<P3>+…]]`

- **Primary tokens**: `SP1 SP3 SP4 SP5 SP6 SP7 SP8 SP9 SP10 PatternN Mechanical`. There is **no live `SP2`** — the legacy `sub-pattern 2` (OMCL 2026-04-29) maps to its taxonomy placement **`SP1`**. `PatternN` is written **without a space** (the negative-direction cross-section / information-confirmation family; predates the numeric scheme).
- **Variant letters** are appended directly to the primary, ONLY where the taxonomy assigns one: `SP4a SP4b SP4c SP4d SP4e SP4f` and `SP5a SP5b SP5c SP5d`. Entries the taxonomy leaves "variant-letter unpinned" stay at the bare primary (e.g. HIMS → `SP4`).
- **Candidate** patterns carry a trailing ` (candidate)`: currently `SP5c (candidate)` (FIG lockup-supply binary), `SP5d (candidate)` (CLSK BTC-commodity binary), `SP9 (candidate)` (UBER split-sell-side), `SP10 (candidate)` (MRNA sign-ambiguous-event / momentum-contaminated move — established 2026-07-19 W5).
- **Overlays / composites**: the **binding/decisive layer is the PRIMARY (written first)**; layered sub-patterns are bracketed as ` [overlay: …]`, joined with `+` — e.g. `SP4f [overlay: SP1+SP3]` (ARM), `SP1 [overlay: SP3]` (layered-1+3), `SP6 [overlay: SP4c+PatternN]` (LULU), `SP4c [overlay: SP4a+SP4b+PatternN]` (BSX). **Direction (positive/negative) is NOT encoded in the token** — it lives in the instance row.
- **Mechanical-Failure** entries (pure archive, NOT a criterion-4 sub-pattern) use `Mechanical (<gate>)`. The four gates in use: `Mechanical (criterion-1 magnitude gate)`, `Mechanical (instrument-rule mcap-floor gate)`, `Mechanical (event-verification gate)`, `Mechanical (sector-cap gate)`.
- **`NULL`** is reserved for entries with no criterion-4 sub-pattern (GO / non-NO-GO session summaries, position/calendar entries) **and** for criterion-4 NO-GOs the taxonomy declines to place (flagged for review).

To group by primary pattern: `REGEXP_EXTRACT(sub_pattern, r'^(SP[0-9]+|PatternN|Mechanical)')`. Per-ticker NO-GO counts: filter `decision='NO-GO' AND ticker IS NOT NULL` (some null-ticker session entries carry an incidental token).

## Sub-Pattern 1 — Aggressive Sell-Side Bull-Ratification

**Mechanism**: Post-event positive-reaction print is met by aggressive sell-side PT raises (often multi-firm, often two-notch upgrades, often street-high revisions) that ratify the print at the higher level. Stock closes at or near the post-raise PT cluster, leaving no asymmetric mean-reversion anchor. SHORT framing fails because sell-side has actively ratified the move (no overshoot for mean-reversion); LONG framing fails because the move is information-driven (PT raises ARE the information confirmation, not sentiment overshoot). Canonical KL #2.20 textbook-rational-trap binding.

**Pattern signal at Daily.md scan stage**: post-print same-day or 48-hour aggressive multi-firm PT raises (≥3 firms with ≥20% PT increases, or single street-high revision ≥50%, or two-notch upgrade with ≥50% PT increase) on a positive-direction event.

**Variants**:
- **Single-firm intermediate intensity**: only one firm posts an aggressive raise (≥+15% PT) but no multi-firm wave develops. Lower-conviction sub-pattern 1 match but still binding on criterion 4.
- **Layered-1+3 (also see Sub-Pattern 3)**: pre-print rally with PT raises (sub-pattern 3) + post-print continuation with additional PT raises (sub-pattern 1). Most extreme magnitude observed when both layers compound.
- **Cleanest-L1 benchmark**: serves as the canonical reference instance for the sub-pattern. Currently CAT (2026-05-02) — replaced prior BE benchmark (2026-05-01).

## Sub-Pattern 3 — Information-Priced-via-Pre-Print-Rally

**Mechanism**: Pre-print PT raises absorb the forward bullish narrative into the print; post-print stock closes AT or modestly above the pre-raised PT cluster mean; sell-side post-print response is mild (PT confirmation rather than aggressive ratification); reaction is information-driven via pre-print rally absorbing expectations. SHORT mean-reversion fails because the move is not overshoot — it is correctly-priced absorption. LONG fails because there is no undershoot anchor (stock is at PT-cluster fair value).

**Pattern signal at Daily.md scan stage**: trailing-30-day +15-20% momentum + pre-print PT raises (≥2 firms with ≥+10% PT raises in 30 days pre-print) + post-print mild sell-side response (≤+5% PT or no changes) + post-print stock at pre-print PT cluster mean.

**Diagnostic signature**: post-print stock closes AT or modestly above the pre-print PT cluster mean. NOT above (would indicate sentiment overshoot — sub-pattern 1 territory); NOT below (would indicate undershoot — LONG mean-reversion candidate).

## Sub-Pattern 4 — Structural-Overhang-Persistence (V/MDLZ Pattern)

**Mechanism**: Post-event reaction is driven by confirmation of a structural overhang that requires multi-quarter resolution outside the 60-day Strategy B window. The overhang is information-driven (print or non-print catalyst CONFIRMS or REVEALS the overhang), not sentiment-driven. LONG mean-reversion fails because the overhang requires multi-quarter resolution; SHORT mean-reversion fails because the information has been broadly absorbed and the stock is no longer in overshoot relative to the new fair-value-with-overhang level.

**Pattern signal at Daily.md scan stage**: print or non-print catalyst surfaces a structural overhang (regulatory, competitive, demand-side, cost-side) requiring multi-quarter resolution; sympathy moves in cross-name peers confirm sector-wide structural-information-driven repricing rather than single-name sentiment overshoot.

**Variants** (subsequent live-entries codified):
- **4a — Pre-existing-overhang-confirmation-by-print**: print confirms previously-flagged overhang (V regulatory; MDLZ cocoa-cost). Guide REAFFIRMED or REVISED with fundamental confirmation. Canonical anchor variant.
- **4b — New-overhang-emergence-at-non-print-catalyst**: structural overhang emerges AT a non-print catalyst event (UPS Amazon Supply Chain Services launch; subsequent NET 2nd instance). **Sub-variant — software/SaaS AI-disruption-restructuring** (NET 2026-05-13 1st instance + INTU 2026-05-26 2nd instance = CONFIRMED): the overhang is an AI-driven competitive-displacement threat to a software/SaaS incumbent, often self-acknowledged via restructuring (workforce cut, "AI-first" pivot rhetoric) and overlaid with a Pattern-N AI-displacement secular read. Route any software/SaaS name where the print/event surfaces AI-substitution risk to the core product here.
- **4c — Guide-cut-on-pre-existing-macro-overhang**: print confirms overhang via hard FY26 guide cut on a pre-existing macro driver (NCLH Iran-war fuel; subsequent SHAK 3rd instance; WHR).
- **4d — Active-securities-fraud-class-action overhang**: structural-litigation overhang (SMCI).
- **4e — New-issuance-dilution overhang** (POET): a dilutive equity raise (registered direct / ATM / secondary issuing NEW shares + warrants) creates a multi-quarter supply / forward-EPS-dilution overhang absorbed outside the 60-day window. **Variant-letter caveat**: the ARM 2026-05-11 session independently self-tagged "4e" for a *supply-side-constraint* overhang — a label collision; POET retains 4e (matches the committed CEG 2026-06-08 reference) and ARM's supply-side-constraint variant was relettered to **4f** (below) to clear the collision.
- **4f — Structural-supply-side-constraint-emergence-at-print** (ARM 2026-05-11): a print reveals a hard, multi-quarter supply / capacity constraint (locked supply ≪ disclosed demand) that caps near-term revenue capture outside the 60-day window — the structural reason a stock can FALL despite a post-print SP1 ratification wave ("stock-failing-to-follow-ratification"). Distinct from 4e (new-issuance dilution).

## Sub-Pattern 5 — In-Window-Binary-Catalyst

**Mechanism**: A binary catalyst event (Investor Day, FDA decision, regulatory ruling, M&A close, stacked near-term binaries) lands INSIDE the 60-day Strategy B post-event window. The pending binary catalyst structurally mismatches Strategy B's mean-reversion narrative-digestion mechanism — narrative cannot stably mean-revert while a binary event in-window introduces step-function repricing. Both LONG and SHORT framings fail because binary asymmetry dominates mean-reversion asymmetry.

**Pattern signal at Daily.md scan stage**: candidate has a binary catalyst (Investor Day, FDA PDUFA, regulatory ruling, M&A close, regulatory markup, election, capital-allocation announcement) scheduled within the 10-day-post-event 60-day Strategy B window.

**Variants**:
- **5a (anchor)**: single in-window binary catalyst (STLA Investor Day).
- **5b — stacked-near-term-binaries**: multiple binary catalysts in-window stacked in week 1-2 of the post-event window (CRCL Q1 + Senate Banking CLARITY-Act markup).
- **5c — lockup-release-as-mechanical-supply-binary** (candidate, FIG 2026-05-18 first instance, pending 2nd-instance validation): a post-IPO share-lockup expiry landing inside the entry window is a step-function mechanical-supply binary that structurally mismatches mean-reversion — distinct from 5a/5b *event* binaries (Investor Day / regulatory).
- **5d — continuous-binary-commodity-exposure** (candidate, CLSK 2026-05-13 first instance, pending 2nd-instance validation): the name is a commodity proxy (e.g., a Bitcoin miner) whose forward 60-day direction is dominated by an unforecastable commodity binary, so there is no company-specific overshoot to mean-revert — the same "binary dominates mean-reversion" mechanism as 5a/5b/5c but CONTINUOUS / un-dated rather than a scheduled event. Often paired with a non-cash mark-to-market accounting distortion (KL #4).
