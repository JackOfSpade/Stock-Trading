## Bear case against the M1b fundamental claim

D's technical rule is not a decorative gate — it is deliberately loose. It fires on `(SPY Trend = UP OR NEUTRAL) AND Sustained Inversion = NOT-SUSTAINED`, admitting two of three possible trend states and requiring only the absence of a specific, well-defined recession precursor (a sustained yield-curve inversion). A rule built that permissively was evidently designed on the assumption that monthly fundamental narrative would disagree with it often — a strategy meant to catch multi-year entries cannot be gated shut every time a macro commentary turns cautious, or it would rarely fire at all. Judged against that design intent, the fundamental case for blocking D has to clear a real bar, and on inspection each of its three legs is thinner than "three headwind legs simultaneously present" suggests. The growth leg is the same input that flipped the other direction just one month ago and produced June's raw ACTIVATE — a leg that reverses direction in successive readings is doing more to demonstrate noise at monthly frequency than to establish a structural condition that should gate a multi-year commitment. The rate leg is doing quiet, undisclosed work: the specific rule that fired last month (`inflation_trend = reaccelerating AND policy_stance = hawkish`) is now conceded inert, and rather than let the DNA case shrink to two legs, the reasoning substitutes a differently-shaped justification — long-rate *level* rather than inflation *trend* — with no stated threshold for how large a monthly rate move has to be before it "compresses long-duration equity value" enough to matter. A 24bp/30bp move is real but not self-evidently extreme, and the artifact supplies no comparator. And the shock leg, while scored `acute` for the month, is shown by the artifact's own text to already be wobbling by August 2 (a stood-down strike, a 5–7% oil give-back) — a geopolitical state that can reverse within days is doing outsized duty against a strategy whose holding period is years. Strip away the labels and what remains is one integrated macro read — the same "stagflationary shock + hawkish policy" characterization M1a stamps across the whole roster — decomposed into three restatements for D rather than three independently corroborating findings, exactly the pattern the artifact's own common-cause warning describes for the roster as a whole. None of that is disqualifying on its own, but together it should give the orchestrator real pause before treating this DNA as materially stronger than the technical ACTIVATE it overrides.
