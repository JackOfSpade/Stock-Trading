## Weaknesses identified

1. **[Tier 1] "CaR is exactly known, not assumed" is contradicted by the artifact's own early-assignment residual** — The rev-10 2.18 mitigation claims C's Capital at Risk is precise and non-assumption-dependent: "That exactness makes the CaR NUMBER precise... C is the one strategy where the size decision's downside is exactly known at entry, not assumed." But `max_loss` (which the artifact defines CaR to equal) is, for any structure with a short leg, computed *inclusive of the bounded early-assignment cascade*, and Known Limitation 9 states this cascade computation itself rests on an assumption that can fail: "the 2× implied-move adverse-move assumption is a model parameter; actual adverse moves can exceed 2× in extreme regimes." A number whose own known-limitations section documents that the underlying assumption can be exceeded is not "exactly known... not assumed" — the artifact asserts the opposite of what it elsewhere concedes. Since this exactness claim is the load-bearing premise distinguishing C's sizing control from the equity strategies' ("undiscounted full notional"), and since the entire two-layer accounting (10%/75% envelopes bounding an "exact" number) depends on that number actually being a hard bound, this is not cosmetic — it is the central quantity the cycle-10 redraft was supposed to get right.
   **ANCHOR:** "That exactness makes the CaR NUMBER precise. It does **not** make the CHOICE of how much CaR to spend..." (2.18 entry) vs. "the 2× implied-move adverse-move assumption is a model parameter; actual adverse moves can exceed 2× in extreme regimes" (Known Limitation 9).

2. **[Tier 1] Gating criteria the revision's own rationale depends on are never defined in the artifact — self-containment failure** — The revision header states cycle 9 found W4 "written entirely in terms of the retired flat 2% cap — screens (i) and (iv)," and elsewhere the artifact conditions authority to widen strategy scope on being "Tier 1 clean (or deployment-risk-acceptable per the four screens)." Nowhere in this artifact are the four screens, or what "(i)" and "(iv)" test, enumerated or defined. Without that definition there is no way to verify from the artifact's own text whether rev 10's 2.18 rewrite actually satisfies whatever screens (i) and (iv) require, or whether "deployment-risk-acceptable per the four screens" is a meaningful bar or an unfalsifiable escape hatch. Per this review's self-containment rule, a needed fact absent from both the artifact and the trigger_context is itself a Tier 1 defect.
   **ANCHOR:** "W4 (the 2.18 mitigation written entirely in terms of the retired flat 2% cap — screens (i) and (iv))" and "Tier 1 clean (or deployment-risk-acceptable per the four screens)."

3. **[Tier 1] Empirical effectiveness figure borrowed for a mechanism it was never measured on** — The rev-10 2.18 entry justifies the newly-added mandatory "adversarial attack on the size" by citing the *thesis*-validity counter-argument's measured effectiveness: "per the same closure this pre-mortem already documents for its own counter-argument entry criterion at Constraint 2 (~30% empirical reduction, ~70% residual) — not eliminative." The 30%/70% figures were derived from data on whether Claude's counter-argument catches an over-fit *thesis*; the size-attack is a brand-new, never-executed requirement (introduced in this same revision) discriminating a different judgment (how much to risk, not whether the thesis holds). Presenting the size-attack's residual in the same numeric terms as a measured, unrelated control implies an empirical basis for its reliability that does not exist — an unverifiable frequency declaration by false equivalence, precisely the pattern this review's instructions flag as canonical Tier 1 theater.
   **ANCHOR:** "per the same closure this pre-mortem already documents for its own counter-argument entry criterion at Constraint 2 (~30% empirical reduction, ~70% residual) — not eliminative."

4. **[Tier 2] Inner judgment layer has no described independent check** — The inner layer (AI-chosen risk budget, seven-factor decision-log justification, size-attack) is disciplined entirely by artifacts the same model instance produces in the same session: the decision-log entry and the counter-argument. No verification, audit, or independent-routine review of whether the seven factors were genuinely engaged (versus checkbox-recorded) is described anywhere in this text — unlike the pre-mortem review process itself, which uses a separate Attacker/Orchestrator pair. The artifact concedes the size-attack is "a same-model check on a same-model decision," which is honest, but that concession does not supply the missing external check; it just names the gap. Given that this inner layer now does materially more of 2.18's (and, per the confluence list, 2.4/2.13/2.15's) compensating work than under the retired fixed cap, the absence of any check external to the sizing model is a material degradation, not merely a disclosed limitation.
   **ANCHOR:** "The adversarial size-attack is a same-model check on a same-model decision, graded... not eliminative."

5. **[Tier 2] Cumulative multi-trade residual left unquantified despite the mechanism's per-trade unit changing** — Known Limitation 10 (cumulative cross-structure adherence drift) was calibrated against an implicit small, flat per-trade increment; the sizing unit is now a variable, judgment-set budget up to 5x larger per trade.    **ANCHOR:** "(Known Limitation 10, not re-quantified by this revision — see RESIDUAL.)"

6. **[Tier 3] "RESIDUAL" is cited as a specific target with no corresponding section** — Five separate places point to "RESIDUAL" as if it were a defined section holding the open completeness question ("recorded in RESIDUAL," "see RESIDUAL"), but no heading or section titled RESIDUAL exists anywhere in this artifact. This makes the pointer itself unverifiable within the document — a reader cannot confirm from the artifact whether the "open completeness question" for 2.4/2.13/2.15 is tracked anywhere concrete or is simply asserted to be tracked.

## Reasoning

The cycle's central question is whether the two-layer replacement for the retired flat 2% cap is honestly characterized: a mechanical-but-looser outer envelope, and a judgment-dependent inner budget. The artifact is unusually candid about the inner layer being weaker and judgment-exposed — that candor is itself evidence against theater, and I did not manufacture a finding out of the honest "same-model check on a same-model decision" admission alone (finding 4 is about the absence of a compensating external check, not about the honesty of the disclosure). What tips the verdict to Tier 1 is that the one place the artifact reaches for a *precise, quantitative* claim about the new control — CaR exactness — is flatly contradicted by its own Known Limitation 9 a few hundred words away, and the one place it reaches for an *empirical* reliability figure for a brand-new mechanism, it borrows a number measured on a different mechanism. Both are the artifact asserting more certainty than its own text supports, which is exactly the "claim of equivalence... not actually supported" and "unverifiable frequency declaration" patterns this review is charged to catch. The undefined "four screens" is a harder self-containment failure: I cannot verify that rev 10 actually closes cycle 9's W4 finding when the very success criteria cycle 9 applied (screens i and iv) are never stated in the text I am permitted to read.
