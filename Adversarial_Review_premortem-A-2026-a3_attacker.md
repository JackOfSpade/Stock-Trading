### W2 — Tier 1 — The cap's stated protective property is structurally incompatible with thesis-scaled CaR, and no replacement property is derived
**Anchor:** "It separates sizing from the narrative output's apparent strength and applies uniformly to all theses regardless of bias mechanism." (08_pre_mortems.md:263)

The mitigating power the artifact claims for the 2% cap is specifically its *independence from narrative strength* — sizing does not vary with how convincing (or biased-convincing) the thesis sounds, so 2.4/2.13/2.14/2.19-driven overconfidence cannot inflate position size. A per-thesis Capital-at-Risk budget that the AI sets against a seven-factor list is, by construction, the opposite of this: sizing now explicitly is a function of the AI's own assessment of the thesis. That assessment is produced by the same weights that 2.4 (over-fit confidence), 2.13 (asymmetric optimism), 2.14 (recency-driven conviction), and 2.19 (buried-framing-driven conviction) already bias. The property the artifact relies on to call this a "flowing limitation" — uniformity and narrative-independence — does not survive the swap to thesis-scaled sizing, and the document does not derive what (if anything) replaces it. Simply noting that <=10% per-name / <=75% per-strategy-deployed envelopes exist would not resolve this: those are outer bounds on the discretionary budget, not a restoration of narrative-independence for the budget-setting step itself.
