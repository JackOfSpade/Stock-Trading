But Section 1's own canonical statement of entry criterion 4 — the section the self-containment statement identifies as enumerating "entry criteria" — reads: *"4. Adversarial counter-argument doesn't identify a decisive flaw — specifically, attacker must consider whether the market reaction is information-driven rather than sentiment-driven... Per Section 5 binding-constraint Constraint 1 below: the counter-argument check is a graded discriminator with ~30% empirical bias-reduction magnitude, not a clean filter."* Nothing in that text instructs an attacker to examine size at all. The inner layer's only claimed judgment-side discipline besides the seven-factor record is therefore asserted in Section 5 but never actually instantiated in the mechanism itself, and the imported 30%/70% figure — measured for a direction-bias question — is applied to an unmeasured size-bias question with no independent support.
   **Anchor:** "the mandatory adversarial attack on the size (entry criterion 4, extended to attack size as well as direction)" (Section 5, cross-constraint inner layer) vs. entry criterion 4's actual text in Section 1, which contains no size language.

## Reasoning

The cycle's central question is whether the two-layer control (mechanical outer envelope, judgment-dependent inner budget) genuinely re-mitigates what the retired flat 2% cap covered, or merely re-labels the same exposure. The artifact does a creditable job of *disclosing* that the inner layer is judgment-dependent and therefore bias-exposed rather than claiming false equivalence with the old mechanical cap — the Residual paragraph is explicit that "that property is gone and is not recovered by CaR sizing." I did not count that honest disclosure itself as a defect; an artifact that admits a residual is behaving correctly, and I deliberately did not treat the acknowledged 5×-larger-blast-radius or the acknowledged ~30%-worked-example deviation as defects in themselves, since they are stated as residuals, not hidden.

What tips this to Tier 1 is that in three specific places the artifact goes further than honest disclosure and asserts a *specific mitigating mechanism or equivalence* that its own text does not support: (1) it names "entry criterion 4, extended to attack size" as the inner layer's judgment-side discipline, but the entry criterion as actually written in Section 1 was never extended, so the inner layer's non-record-keeping discipline is asserted rather than real; (2) it leans on an external, unreproduced finding (AI_Edges A2 2026) and an undefined disadvantage number (2.26) to compound the residual's severity, which I cannot verify and the artifact does not entitle me to accept on faith, especially since Known Limitation #4 demonstrates the authors know how to inline an external finding correctly when they choose to; and (3) it claims a specific lagging-indicator fix "restores" a discrimination property that its own stated numbers (5+ positions required before, only 2 required now) show it does not restore.

I deliberately did not flag the Section 2/3/5 pre-existing residuals (narrative over-fit, homogenization, base-rate neglect, etc.) as defects — the trigger context states these are the cycle-7 Tier 2/3 residuals (W4–W7) explicitly out of scope for this redraft, and I have no way to know their content under blinding, so I confined my attack to material rev-8 actually touched: the CaR sizing re-derivation, the two-layer control framing, the KL #12 worked example, and the Section 6/KL #12 monitoring-trigger recalibration. I also verified the KL #12 arithmetic itself (6 × 10% × 50% = 30%, consistently re-deriving the same formula the pre-Rev-43 6% figure used) and found it internally consistent, so I did not flag it as a defect despite initially suspecting a mismatched-basis problem between the two "−50%" figures used elsewhere in the document.
