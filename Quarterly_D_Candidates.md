1. Methodology and Caveats
Candidates were sourced from a broad cross-sector scan using SEC EDGAR filings (10-Ks, 10-Qs, 8-Ks, DEF 14A), company investor-relations pages (earnings transcripts and investor-day decks), and reputable financial press (CNBC, Reuters/Bloomberg, SEC filings, industry trade press). All names screened meet Strategy D's baseline eligibility: US-listed common equity (or ADR for large-cap foreign domiciles), market cap ≥ $10B, and 30-day ADV ≥ $20M (all names in the long list are S&P 500 / large-cap constituents trading >$100M/day on typical sessions).
Per Strategy D's pre-mortem, I explicitly avoided generic "AI theme exposure" rationales (pre-mortem 2.4 — AI narrative over-fit) and required each candidate to reference a specific, checkable, multi-year structural driver with primary-source evidence. Trailing-30-day price performance was checked against published closing-price data as of the April 22, 2026 close; any name up >15% in the trailing 30 days was flagged for entry deferral.
