   **ANCHOR CONVENTION — `qualifying_event_date` IS THE DATE THE EVENT BECAME PUBLIC, NEVER THE SESSION THE MOVE WAS MEASURED IN (added 2026-09-21 by D1, closing its own `d1_qualifying_event_date_anchor_unspecified`, `ops.alerts` info `03b8f773`, 2026-09-16).** The rule already existed — `Watchlist.md`'s **ANCHOR PIN** (D2 2026-08-19) states "the event date governs; the flag-date wording above is legacy" — but it was written **only inside D2's section and that file**, and **D1, which is the routine that actually SETS the field, carried no equivalent limb.** The consequence is measured, not hypothetical: on **four consecutive cycles** D1's anchor had to be corrected downstream at D2 — HPE/DELL right on 2026-09-13, JBHT wrong on 2026-09-16, SMR wrong on 2026-09-17, and **all five of the 2026-09-20 cohort wrong** (XENE, NUE, MSTR, COIN re-anchored 09-18 → 09-17; BE re-anchored 09-18 → 09-04, on which its entry window had **already closed**).
   - **The rule, stated operationally.** The anchor is the date the qualifying information CROSSED THE WIRE. A release that lands **after the close** of session S anchors on **S**, and the reaction you measure on S+1 is the *reaction session*, not the anchor — that is the single case D1 has gotten wrong every time, because the reaction session is the one the screen's own close-to-close arithmetic lives in. A release public **intraday** on session S anchors on S, and the ≥5% test is then measured on S itself. A release **pre-open** on session S anchors on S. Record BOTH dates in the item — `qualifying_event_date` (the anchor) and the reaction session the `prior_close`/`event_close` pair was taken from — so a later reader can see the two are deliberately different rather than suspecting a typo.
   - **THE ANCHOR AND THE MEASUREMENT ARE TWO CLOCKS — MEASURE CRITERION 1 ON THE REACTION SESSION, ANCHOR THE WINDOW ON THE RELEASE (added 2026-10-18, W5 SPEC-DEFECT NOTICE INTAKE, closing D1's `d1_anchor_convention_magnitude_clock_conflated`, alert `06c3b0db-2d58-44c4-a53a-7474cd32b57f`).** This limb forbids ANCHORING on the reaction session; it does NOT forbid MEASURING the ≥5% magnitude there. For an AFTER-CLOSE release the anchor is session S (it fixes the entry-window start and the 10-trading-day count) while the first session able to price the information is S+1, so `metric_pct`, `prior_close`/`event_close` and `below_spec_floor` are taken on the REACTION session S+1; for an intraday or pre-open release the two coincide on S.
