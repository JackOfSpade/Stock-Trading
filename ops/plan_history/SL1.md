STEP 3 — MECHANICAL QUALIFICATION (DEFAULT-REJECT on any unmet rail or ambiguity). For each NEW candidate, in order — fail any → REJECTED:
   (a) **Cited-edge validity.** Every thesis rule cites a valid live `AI_Trading_Foundation.md` edge/disadvantage and the candidate names a compensated non-version-pending Part-2 disadvantage. A scope refinement, contradiction, version-pending magnitude, or guardrail-failing reduction is NOT compensation and cannot be routed as relaxed. A research-grade source label or upstream seed does not exempt the candidate. The return-generating mechanism itself must trace to a Part-1 edge; an edge used only to detect/select a classical factor wrapper is insufficient. If a named prospective detector/signal, target cells, frequency, invalidation, or kill structure cannot be derived, fail on the merits under default-REJECT rather than treating the fields as merely unfilled. **SHOCK-NATIVE clause (Rev 50, owner directive 2026-10-19).** A candidate whose activation rule REQUIRES `shock_overlay ∈ {latent, acute}` is a SHOCK-NATIVE class under Strategy.md's Shock Transmission Register and is exempt from the acute override by construction, but qualifies ONLY if all three Rev 50 authoring rails hold — (i) hedged or defined-risk structure with no net-long directional expression; (ii) per-thesis invalidation keyed to the `### Shock / overlay grading rubric`'s symmetric exit test (re-pointed by Rev 52, owner directive 2026-10-22, interactive session, from the retired 15-trading-day quiet window and 50% retracement: the price leg reading NOT ELEVATED — Brent below the baseline-to-peak midpoint, or its elevation zeroed by a plateau re-base — with no sovereign-spread or FX leg ACTIVE, or a qualifying resolution event closing the structure); (iii) declared frequency denominated in qualifying shock events — default-REJECT if any is unmet or ambiguous.
   (b) **Material-structural-difference vs every live AND every terminated strategy** — differs in >=1 of {approach, instrument scope, sizing methodology, router structure, kill-criteria structure} beyond threshold numbers (the Experiment_Parameters.md restart constraints, now machine-enforced). For a restart (`is_restart_of` set): the terminated strategy's `events.strategy_postmortems` row MUST exist AND its `material_diff_required_for_restart` field must articulate the difference; require that a restart is never proposed without a post-mortem (the fresh-derivation byte-match check is applied by SL2/AR against `derivation_provenance`).
   (c) **Non-redundancy / differentiation vs the current roster** — not dominated by a live strategy on its target regime cells.
   (d) **Capacity/cost fit** at the dynamic ramp floor (rev 20, 2026-10-19: `GREATEST($2,000 cost minimum, 2% of book NAV)` — `state.strategy_ramp.floor_usd`'s formula; $2,000 at today's book): modeled all-in round-trip commission as a percentage of deployed notional must be `<= state.arsenal_rails.max_roundtrip_commission_pct` (=1.8%, the current accepted probe-scale ceiling). Frequency is reported separately as annual-dollar drag; do not multiply a percentage of per-trade notional by frequency and compare it to a per-round-trip ceiling. Include borrow and spread/slippage when the instrument requires them; default-REJECT if executable size or a fillable price cannot be verified.
   (e) **Ceiling clear** — roster active-count below `n_max`.
   (f) **Cooldown clear** — no same-archetype `cooldown_until` in force (post-rejection / post-termination stamp).
   (g) **Ramp declaration (rev 20, owner directive 2026-10-19 — LIVE_RAMP_DESIGN.md sections 3 and 5).** Derive and write to `state.strategy_candidates` the four parameters the live ramp runs on — `declared_mu_per_trade` (expected net return per closed campaign, a fraction of cost basis, > 0), `declared_sigma_per_trade` (> 0), `declared_tail_loss_5pct` (< 0; NULL means mu − 1.645·sigma), `declared_holding_horizon_days` — and score the OPENING-SCORE RUBRIC: seven checkable components, each 0 / 0.5 / 1 — backtest strength, evidence breadth, harness coverage of the judgment layer, mechanism class, cost margin at the floor, adversarial-review cost, redundancy with the live roster — with `ramp_opening_score = 0.4 × mean(components)`, floored at 0 when there is neither a backtest nor a prospective paper book, and the components written as `ramp_rubric_json`. Never a self-reported confidence: every component names the evidence it was scored from. A backtest counts only when contamination-safe (post-cutoff window, or masked tickers and dates, placebo-controlled); a mechanism with no clean backtest still qualifies — it deploys at the floor and earns its ramp live. Default-REJECT if `mu` or `sigma` cannot be derived from the thesis's own declared magnitude and frequency. Frequency (rail (a)'s SHOCK-NATIVE (iii), `declared_annual_roundtrips`) is reported, never gated against a trade-count graduation: there is no 30-trade gate (Experiment_Parameters.md rev 20).
