## 1. Problem

The Strategy Arsenal Lifecycle (SL1-SL5) gates every adoption and retirement on a two-routine adversarial
review — `AR_att` (attacker) vs `AR_orc` (orchestrator) — plus, since `bigquery/11_theater_judge.sql`, a
retrospective LLM judge that scores whether the orchestrator's transcript shows *independent* reasoning
from the attacker's, replacing the raw cosine similarity that `bigquery/04_analytics.sql` found
"topically saturated ~0.95-0.97 ... NOT a sharp CONVERGENT detector" across all three historical reviews
regardless of theater-check flag.

Two separate weaknesses remain, both already conceded in the docs and neither fixed by the theater judge:

1. **The attacker and the orchestrator are generated by the same model family.** Both `AR_att` and
   `AR_orc` run as Claude sessions with only context-window isolation (`AI_Trading_Foundation.md` 2.24:
   "Separate sessions with isolated context genuinely produce different reasoning... as long as their
   contexts are separated" — the edge is real but bounded by shared weights, shared RLHF-induced priors,
   shared blind spots). `AlphaForgeBench` (arXiv 2602.18481, cited at 2.24) shows even temperature=0
   decoding produces divergent *action sequences* on identical inputs — session isolation buys stochastic
   diversity, not epistemic independence. This is the AlphaForgeBench / "same-weights" disadvantage the
   item cites (2.24's own framing: "long-horizon strategic consistency cannot be expected from the model
   alone").
2. **`bigquery/11_theater_judge.sql`'s Gemini judge only grades the TEXT** the two same-family sessions
   already produced (`judge_independent BOOL` = "did the orchestrator's transcript show independent
   reasoning" per `analytics.theater_check_calibration`). It never forms its own substantive verdict on
   the underlying decision. A Claude orchestrator that cleverly disguises an echo of the attacker as
   "independent-sounding" prose could still pass; the judge is retrospective style-scoring, not a second
   opinion on the merits.

Both weaknesses matter most on **irreversible-cost decisions** — a strategy adoption commits future PROBE
capital and roster-ceiling headroom; a strategy retirement or a foundation-change TERMINATE ends a live
strategy's measurement and (per `Experiment_Parameters.md`:507) forces any restart back through the full
graduation pipeline from CANDIDATE. They matter far less on cheap-to-reverse internal transitions
(SHADOW→PAPER, PAPER→PROBE) which are already gated by *objective, non-LLM* signals (`shadow_days`,
`sim_closed_trades`, `excess >= 0`, regime coverage — see `state.strategy_shadow_readiness` /
`state.strategy_paper_readiness` in `bigquery/35_strategy_arsenal.sql`) rather than an adversarial verdict
at all. This spike is therefore scoped narrowly.

## 2. Scope — IRREVERSIBLE decisions only

| Decision | Current gate | Irreversible because |
|---|---|---|
| Strategy adoption (`UNDER_REVIEW`→`SHADOW`) | `state.strategy_adoption_readiness`, AR_orc `strategy-adoption` verdict = SUFFICIENT | Commits `k_incubate` / `n_max` headroom; a wrong SUFFICIENT occupies a roster slot for the full graduation pipeline before it can be discovered wrong |
| Strategy retirement (`RETIREMENT_PROPOSED`→`TERMINATED`) | AR_orc `strategy-retirement` verdict = RETIRE, read directly by SL5 (no dedicated SQL readiness view exists today — see §4.3) | Ends live measurement; restart re-enters as a fresh CANDIDATE (`Experiment_Parameters.md`:507), full pipeline again |
| Foundation-change TERMINATE (per-strategy `foundation-change-assessment`, `Claude_Task_Plan.md`:224) | A **single Claude session** drains the `PENDING_ANALYSIS` queue entry and writes one `events.decision_log` row — no AR pair, no `events.adversarial_reviews` row at all today | Same as retirement, and currently has the *weakest* scrutiny of the three (not even same-family adversarial review) |

Explicitly OUT of scope (unchanged): SHADOW→PAPER, PAPER→PROBE (objective-signal gates, no LLM verdict in
the loop), `out-of-table-resolution` (default-HOLD, i.e. defaults to *not* changing anything — the
fail-safe direction already), and `m2m-termination` / drawdown / runaway-success (already mechanical or
already AR-gated at the same bar as retirement — folding those in is a natural follow-on, not blocking
this spike).

## 3. Design

**"Materially different model family" is already satisfied by what is provisioned.** `ops.gemini`
(`bigquery/02_ai_layer.sql`) is a `REMOTE MODEL ... WITH CONNECTION stock-trading-498512.us.vertex`,
`ENDPOINT = 'gemini-2.5-flash'` — Google DeepMind Gemini, a different lab, different pretraining corpus,
different RLHF pipeline, different architecture family than Anthropic Claude. This is not a new
integration; `bigquery/11_theater_judge.sql` already calls it today. The gap item 27 identifies is not
"we lack a second model family" — it is "the second model family only judges STYLE after the fact; it
never forms its own SUBSTANTIVE verdict that gates the decision."

**The fix: a third `events.adversarial_reviews` role, `referee_gemini`, that independently DECIDES, not
just grades.** `events.adversarial_reviews.role` is an unconstrained `STRING` (`bigquery/01_schema.sql`) —
adding a new role value is a zero-migration, backward-compatible change; every existing `role = 'attacker'`
/ `role = 'orchestrator'` filter in `bigquery/35_strategy_arsenal.sql` and `bigquery/11_theater_judge.sql`
is unaffected because they filter ON role, not against an enum.

- The referee reads **only the attacker's case** (`role = 'attacker'`), never the orchestrator's verdict
  or reasoning. This is a genuine blind — not the theater judge's design (which deliberately shows both
  transcripts to grade their relationship) but the opposite: the referee must reach its OWN verdict before
  it can be compared to the orchestrator's, or "independence" is measured against contaminated priors.
- It runs via the exact same `AI.GENERATE_TABLE(MODEL ops.gemini, ...)` pattern
  `ops.sp_score_theater()` already uses, so the operational shape (on-demand procedure, MERGE-based,
  `temperature=0.0`, `WHERE NOT EXISTS` to only score unscored rows) is a direct copy, not a new pattern to
  operate.
- Its verdict is stored as an ordinary `events.adversarial_reviews` row (`role = 'referee_gemini'`), so it
  inherits the append-only, audit-trail-by-construction property every other adversarial output already
  has — no new table needed for the review record itself.
- Readiness views add an **AND-condition**: the existing verdict AND the referee's verdict must **concur**
  (same directional outcome — SUFFICIENT/SUFFICIENT, RETIRE/RETIRE, TERMINATE/TERMINATE). A referee row
  that is absent, still pending, or that DISAGREES all read as `NOT ready` — fail-closed, per hard
  constraint #3. Disagreement does not raise an exception or ask a question; it simply leaves the
  transition un-fired, exactly like every other unmet readiness component today (e.g. `shadow_days < 20`).
  The strategy stays in its current state until the *next* firing re-evaluates the view — no different
  from any other readiness gate that hasn't cleared yet.

## 4. What is implementable TODAY (existing `ops.gemini` / Vertex substrate, zero new credentials)

### 4.1 `ops.sp_score_cross_model_referee()` — the referee procedure

Direct structural analog of `ops.sp_score_theater()` (`bigquery/11_theater_judge.sql`), but it generates a
fresh verdict from the attacker's case alone instead of grading two transcripts' relationship. Scoped to
exactly the three irreversible review types via `WHERE a.review_type IN (...)`.

```sql
-- ops.sp_score_cross_model_referee() — SPEC SKETCH, validated by BigQuery dryRun against the live
-- schema (events.adversarial_reviews, ops.gemini) on 2026-07-11; not applied.
CREATE OR REPLACE PROCEDURE `stock-trading-498512.ops.sp_score_cross_model_referee`()
BEGIN
  MERGE `stock-trading-498512.events.adversarial_reviews` T
  USING (
    SELECT
      g.review_id, 'referee_gemini' AS role, g.review_type, g.strategy, g.review_date, g.cycle_number,
      g.verdict_out AS verdict,
      CAST(NULL AS STRING) AS theater_check,
      TO_JSON(STRUCT(g.reasoning AS reasoning)) AS weaknesses,
      CAST(NULL AS STRING) AS artifact_path,
      g.reasoning AS body_md
    FROM AI.GENERATE_TABLE(
      MODEL `stock-trading-498512.ops.gemini`,
      (
        SELECT a.review_id, a.review_type, a.strategy, a.review_date, a.cycle_number,
          CONCAT(
            'You are an INDEPENDENT cross-model referee for an IRREVERSIBLE autonomous-trading-system ',
            'decision. You have NOT seen any other reviewer opinion or verdict -- form your own from ',
            'first principles against the case below only. Return the SAME verdict vocabulary the review ',
            'type uses (SUFFICIENT/INSUFFICIENT for strategy-adoption; RETIRE/KEEP for strategy-retirement; ',
            'TERMINATE/CONTINUE/CONSTRAINT_RELAXATION for foundation-change-assessment). Default on genuine ',
            'ambiguity: ',
            CASE a.review_type
              WHEN 'strategy-adoption' THEN 'INSUFFICIENT (reject)'
              WHEN 'strategy-retirement' THEN 'KEEP'
              ELSE 'CONTINUE' END,
            '.\n\nReview type: ', a.review_type,
            '\n\n=== CASE (attacker submission only -- no orchestrator text shown) ===\n',
            SUBSTR(COALESCE(a.body_md,''), 1, 6000)
          ) AS prompt
        FROM `stock-trading-498512.events.adversarial_reviews` a
        WHERE a.role = 'attacker'
          AND a.review_type IN ('strategy-adoption','strategy-retirement','foundation-change-assessment')
          AND NOT EXISTS (
            SELECT 1 FROM `stock-trading-498512.events.adversarial_reviews` r
            WHERE r.review_id = a.review_id AND r.role = 'referee_gemini')
      ),
      STRUCT('verdict_out STRING, reasoning STRING' AS output_schema, 0.0 AS temperature)
    ) g
  ) S
  ON T.review_id = S.review_id AND T.role = S.role
  WHEN NOT MATCHED THEN INSERT
    (review_id, role, review_type, strategy, review_date, cycle_number, verdict, theater_check, weaknesses, artifact_path, body_md)
    VALUES (S.review_id, S.role, S.review_type, S.strategy, S.review_date, S.cycle_number, S.verdict, S.theater_check, S.weaknesses, S.artifact_path, S.body_md);
END;
```

Notes:
- `foundation-change-assessment` rows do not exist in `events.adversarial_reviews` yet (today it is a
  single-session `PENDING_ANALYSIS` drain, `Claude_Task_Plan.md`:224, that writes only to
  `events.decision_log`). Making the referee reachable for that review type requires the routine itself to
  ALSO write an `events.adversarial_reviews` `role='orchestrator'` row (`review_type =
  'foundation-change-assessment'`, `verdict IN ('CONTINUE','TERMINATE','CONSTRAINT_RELAXATION')`) before a
  TERMINATE outcome executes. That is a `Claude_Task_Plan.md` procedure-text change, not a new credential
  or model integration — flagged here for completeness, out of this spike's `shared_edits` (scoped to
  RUNBOOK §39 + `autonomy_levels.yaml` only) and left for the routine-text follow-on.
- Cost: identical shape to `ops.sp_score_theater()`, i.e. negligible (`gemini-2.5-flash`, a handful of
  reviews per quarter at most given the `adoption_rate_window_days=90` / retirement-cooldown rails).

### 4.2 Readiness-view AND-condition — `state.strategy_adoption_readiness` patch

Adds one `LEFT JOIN` + one boolean term to the existing view (`bigquery/35_strategy_arsenal.sql` lines
377-404). `LEFT JOIN` (not `JOIN`) + `COALESCE(...,'MISSING')` is what makes a missing referee row read as
`NOT ready` rather than dropping the strategy out of the result set — the fail-closed requirement.

```sql
-- PATCH SKETCH for state.strategy_adoption_readiness (full CREATE OR REPLACE VIEW omitted; this is the
-- delta against bigquery/35_strategy_arsenal.sql's existing CTEs + SELECT list + WHERE). Validated by
-- BigQuery dryRun against the live schema on 2026-07-11 (0 bytes billed); not applied.
...
referee AS (
  SELECT strategy AS strategy_code, verdict AS referee_verdict
  FROM `stock-trading-498512.events.adversarial_reviews`
  WHERE review_type = 'strategy-adoption' AND role = 'referee_gemini'
  QUALIFY ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY strategy ORDER BY review_date DESC, event_ts DESC) = 1
),
...
SELECT
  r.strategy_code,
  v.verdict,
  v.verdict = 'SUFFICIENT' AS review_sufficient,
  COALESCE(ref.referee_verdict, 'MISSING') = 'SUFFICIENT' AS referee_concurs,   -- NEW
  NOT rails.incubation_cap_reached AS caps_ok,
  NOT rails.at_ceiling AS ceiling_ok,
  (ars.enabled AND NOT ars.incubation_frozen) AS arsenal_ok,
  NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM `stock-trading-498512.ops.roster_change_log` cl
              WHERE cl.change_key = CONCAT(r.strategy_code, ':UNDER_REVIEW->SHADOW')) AS not_already_transitioned,
  (v.verdict = 'SUFFICIENT'
   AND COALESCE(ref.referee_verdict, 'MISSING') = 'SUFFICIENT'                 -- NEW AND-term
   AND NOT rails.incubation_cap_reached
   AND NOT rails.at_ceiling
   AND ars.enabled AND NOT ars.incubation_frozen
   AND NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM `stock-trading-498512.ops.roster_change_log` cl
                   WHERE cl.change_key = CONCAT(r.strategy_code, ':UNDER_REVIEW->SHADOW'))) AS ready
FROM `stock-trading-498512.state.strategy_roster` r
JOIN latest_verdict v ON v.strategy_code = r.strategy_code
LEFT JOIN referee ref ON ref.strategy_code = r.strategy_code                    -- NEW
CROSS JOIN rails CROSS JOIN ars
WHERE r.current_state = 'UNDER_REVIEW';
```

### 4.3 NEW `state.strategy_retirement_readiness` view

Today the final retirement TERMINATE step has **no dedicated SQL readiness view** — SL5's TERMINATED-
deregister dispatch reads the AR_orc `strategy-retirement` verdict directly (`Claude_Task_Plan.md`, SL5
step (3): "on a TERMINATED `events.strategy_lifecycle` row from D2 step-5 / AR_orc"). Introducing this view
both closes that gap (bringing retirement onto the same D2a `ready`-boolean pattern as every other
transition, per `ops/RUNBOOK.md` §39 "Same D2a skeleton per transition") AND is the natural place to fold
in the referee AND-condition.

```sql
-- state.strategy_retirement_readiness — SPEC SKETCH. Validated by BigQuery dryRun against the live
-- schema on 2026-07-11 (0 bytes billed); not applied. Would sit in bigquery/35_strategy_arsenal.sql
-- alongside its three siblings (strategy_adoption_readiness / strategy_shadow_readiness /
-- strategy_paper_readiness), same CROSS JOIN rails / ars shape, same NOT-EXISTS idempotency guard.
CREATE OR REPLACE VIEW `stock-trading-498512.state.strategy_retirement_readiness` AS
WITH orch AS (
  SELECT strategy AS strategy_code, verdict AS orch_verdict, review_id,
         ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY strategy ORDER BY review_date DESC, event_ts DESC) AS rn
  FROM `stock-trading-498512.events.adversarial_reviews`
  WHERE review_type = 'strategy-retirement' AND role = 'orchestrator'
),
referee AS (
  SELECT strategy AS strategy_code, verdict AS referee_verdict
  FROM `stock-trading-498512.events.adversarial_reviews`
  WHERE review_type = 'strategy-retirement' AND role = 'referee_gemini'
  QUALIFY ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY strategy ORDER BY review_date DESC, event_ts DESC) = 1
),
rails AS (SELECT * FROM `stock-trading-498512.state.arsenal_rails`),
ars AS (SELECT enabled, incubation_frozen FROM `stock-trading-498512.state.arsenal_enabled`)
SELECT
  r.strategy_code,
  o.orch_verdict,
  ref.referee_verdict,
  COALESCE(o.orch_verdict, 'MISSING') = 'RETIRE' AS orchestrator_retire,
  COALESCE(ref.referee_verdict, 'MISSING') = 'RETIRE' AS referee_concurs,
  rails.active_count > rails.n_min AS floor_ok,
  (ars.enabled AND NOT ars.incubation_frozen) AS arsenal_ok,
  NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM `stock-trading-498512.ops.roster_change_log` cl
              WHERE cl.strategy_code = r.strategy_code AND cl.to_state = 'TERMINATED') AS not_already_transitioned,
  (COALESCE(o.orch_verdict, 'MISSING') = 'RETIRE'
   AND COALESCE(ref.referee_verdict, 'MISSING') = 'RETIRE'
   AND rails.active_count > rails.n_min
   AND ars.enabled AND NOT ars.incubation_frozen
   AND NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM `stock-trading-498512.ops.roster_change_log` cl
                   WHERE cl.strategy_code = r.strategy_code AND cl.to_state = 'TERMINATED')) AS ready
FROM `stock-trading-498512.state.strategy_roster` r
LEFT JOIN orch o ON o.strategy_code = r.strategy_code AND o.rn = 1
LEFT JOIN referee ref ON ref.strategy_code = r.strategy_code
CROSS JOIN rails CROSS JOIN ars
WHERE r.current_state = 'RETIREMENT_PROPOSED';
```

A KEEP verdict is unaffected by this view (KEEP already returns the strategy to ADOPTED via existing
SL4/AR_orc logic without touching this gate at all — it is the fail-safe direction and stays ungated, same
as today). Only the RETIRE-and-actually-terminate path picks up the referee AND-condition. SL5's dispatch
condition for the TERMINATED-deregister branch would read `state.strategy_retirement_readiness.ready`
instead of the raw AR_orc verdict — a `Claude_Task_Plan.md` procedure-text change, out of this spike's
`shared_edits` scope for the same reason as 4.1's note.

### 4.4 NEW `state.foundation_change_termination_readiness` view (depends on 4.1's routine-text note)

Only the TERMINATE branch is irreversible; CONTINUE and CONSTRAINT_RELAXATION are the fail-safe directions
(the strategy keeps running) and stay ungated, exactly like retirement's KEEP path.

```sql
-- state.foundation_change_termination_readiness — SPEC SKETCH. Validated by BigQuery dryRun against
-- the live schema on 2026-07-11 (0 bytes billed); not applied. Depends on the foundation-change-
-- assessment routine writing an events.adversarial_reviews role='orchestrator' row (see 4.1 note) --
-- until that lands, this view returns zero rows (fail-closed by construction, not by a WHERE FALSE).
CREATE OR REPLACE VIEW `stock-trading-498512.state.foundation_change_termination_readiness` AS
WITH orch AS (
  SELECT strategy AS strategy_code, review_id, verdict AS orch_verdict,
         ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY strategy ORDER BY review_date DESC, event_ts DESC) AS rn
  FROM `stock-trading-498512.events.adversarial_reviews`
  WHERE review_type = 'foundation-change-assessment' AND role = 'orchestrator'
),
referee AS (
  SELECT review_id, verdict AS referee_verdict
  FROM `stock-trading-498512.events.adversarial_reviews`
  WHERE review_type = 'foundation-change-assessment' AND role = 'referee_gemini'
)
SELECT
  o.strategy_code, o.review_id, o.orch_verdict, ref.referee_verdict,
  o.orch_verdict = 'TERMINATE' AS orchestrator_terminate,
  COALESCE(ref.referee_verdict, 'MISSING') = 'TERMINATE' AS referee_concurs,
  NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM `stock-trading-498512.ops.roster_change_log` cl
              WHERE cl.strategy_code = o.strategy_code AND cl.to_state = 'TERMINATED'
                AND cl.note LIKE 'foundation-change%') AS not_already_transitioned,
  (o.orch_verdict = 'TERMINATE'
   AND COALESCE(ref.referee_verdict, 'MISSING') = 'TERMINATE'
   AND NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM `stock-trading-498512.ops.roster_change_log` cl
                   WHERE cl.strategy_code = o.strategy_code AND cl.to_state = 'TERMINATED'
                     AND cl.note LIKE 'foundation-change%')) AS ready
FROM orch o
LEFT JOIN referee ref USING (review_id)
WHERE o.rn = 1;
```

## 4b. What requires a NEW provider credential

**Nothing, for the two-family (Anthropic Claude + Google Gemini) design in §3-4.4 above.** `ops.gemini` /
the `us.vertex` `CLOUD_RESOURCE` connection is already provisioned and already billed against
(`bigquery/02_ai_layer.sql`, in production use by `ops.sp_score_theater()` and the ticker backfill since
2026-06-06). Everything in §4.1-4.4 is new SQL objects and (for 4.1/4.3's foundation-change wiring) new
routine text — no new secret, IAM grant, or external account.

The ONLY scenario needing a new credential is going **beyond two families to three**, e.g. if the owner
later wants a referee that is not Vertex-hosted at all (removing any residual "same cloud reseller /
overlapping safety-tuning data" concern between Anthropic-on-Vertex-adjacent-infra and Gemini-on-Vertex —
not applicable today since Claude is not called via BigQuery/Vertex in this system, but flagged for
completeness). Concretely, EITHER of:

- **OpenAI GPT** via a new OpenAI API key + a new BigQuery remote connection/model (the same `REMOTE MODEL
  ... WITH CONNECTION` shape as `ops.gemini`, pointed at an OpenAI-compatible endpoint or via Vertex AI's
  model garden if/when it reseller-hosts a materially-independent third family), OR
- **A self-hosted open-weights model** (e.g. Llama or Mistral) via a new inference-endpoint credential
  (Cloud Run / GKE / a hosted-inference provider) and a new BigQuery remote connection pointed at it.

Neither is required to implement §4.1-4.4. Both are named explicitly in `owner_actions` below purely so
they are visible as a FUTURE option, not a blocker.

## 4c. Confirms NO human gate

- The referee's verdict is generated by an unattended `AI.GENERATE_TABLE` call in a MERGE-based procedure
  (`ops.sp_score_cross_model_referee()`), fired on the exact same cadence discipline as
  `ops.sp_score_theater()` today (on-demand / dead-man-monitored, never a chat question).
- Disagreement or a missing referee row does not raise an alert requiring a human decision — it is read as
  `NOT ready` by the readiness view, i.e. the transition simply does not fire this cycle, identical in
  kind to any other unmet component (`shadow_days < 20`, `at_ceiling`, `incubation_frozen`). The strategy
  stays where it is; SL1-SL5 re-evaluate on their normal cadence.
- No new field routes to a `PENDING_REVIEW` / `PENDING_ANALYSIS` queue, no new calendar event type, no new
  `Confirm-order`-style tap. The only pre-existing human touches (IBKR order-confirm, deposits) are
  unchanged and untouched by this spike.
- The compensating control for a referee failure mode (e.g. Gemini itself drifts, or the Vertex connection
  degrades) is mechanical: the readiness view simply never clears (`referee_concurs` stays FALSE), so a
  broken referee can only ever make the system MORE conservative (block a transition), never less — the
  same fail-safe-direction property every other rail in `bigquery/35_strategy_arsenal.sql` already has
  (`state.arsenal_enabled` failing to `enabled=FALSE` on an empty table; a `NOT EXISTS` idempotency guard
  defaulting to "not yet transitioned"). This is consistent with hard constraint #3 (new gate conditions
  default FALSE/not-ready on missing/NULL data).

## 5. Residual limitation (honest accounting, not resolved by this spike)

Both Claude (attacker/orchestrator) and Gemini (referee) are large models trained substantially on
overlapping public-web/scientific-literature corpora and tuned with broadly similar RLHF objectives
(helpfulness/harmlessness). "Materially different model family" buys architectural and training-pipeline
independence, not full epistemic independence — the same caveat AlphaForgeBench raises for *any* LLM-based
trading decision (2.24). Blinding the referee to the orchestrator's verdict (§3) removes the sharpest
contamination vector (anchoring on a stated conclusion), but does not eliminate correlated priors on, e.g.,
"conservative bias toward REJECT/KEEP looks safe." This is a known, disclosed residual — not a reason to
withhold the fix (a blinded cross-family second opinion is strictly more independent than the status quo,
which has no second opinion on the merits at all), but future revisions (2.24's own recommended direction)
could extend to a genuinely disjoint-corpus / disjoint-RLHF third family per §4b if the owner judges the
residual worth the new credential surface.

## 6. Rollout staging (mirrors `ops/autonomy_levels.yaml` vocabulary; not applied by this spike)

Suggested (not enacted) staging if adopted: `dormant` (objects created, nothing reads/writes through them)
→ `shadow` (procedure runs, referee rows are written and visible in `analytics.theater_check_calibration`-
style reporting, but the readiness-view AND-condition is NOT yet added — burn in referee/orchestrator
concurrence rate over one full quarterly cycle) → `active_auto` (AND-condition live in the readiness
views). This is the SAME "record-only first, then promote by editing the register" discipline
`ops/autonomy_levels.yaml`'s header already documents for every other loop — no new promotion mechanism
needed, and at NO stage does promotion route through a human PR/chat gate; the register file itself is
what a routine (or the owner, per the file's existing convention) edits, citing the observed concurrence
rate as evidence.
