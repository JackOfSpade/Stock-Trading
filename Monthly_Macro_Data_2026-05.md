### Section 5 — Policy environment

**Court of International Trade — May 7, 2026 ruling (Section 122 tariffs):**
- Holding: by 2–1 vote the U.S. Court of International Trade ruled that the 10% (later 15%) global tariff imposed under Section 122 of the Trade Act of 1974 was unlawful. The court found that the statutory conditions required to invoke Section 122 were not satisfied and that the long-running U.S. goods/current-account deficit does not constitute a "fundamental international payments problem" within the statute.
- Procedural posture: relief is a permanent injunction limited to the prevailing plaintiffs (Burlap & Barrel Inc., Basic Fun Inc., State of Washington); all other importers continue to pay Section 122 duties.
- Stay: on May 12, 2026, the Federal Circuit entered an administrative stay suspending the CIT order pending appeal — practical effect of the May 7 ruling on overall U.S. tariff revenue and effective tariff rate is muted while appeal proceeds.
- Source: gibsondunn.com section-122-tariffs-invalidated; bdo.com us-court-of-international-trade-invalidates-section-122; skadden.com 2026/05 us-trade-court-strikes-down; clarkhill.com section-122-tariffs-ruling; dwt.com 2026/05.
